The project

Where a site is already a neighbourhood.

VIP Landmark is a 25-acre residential layout at Kothakote village, Bagepalli taluk, Chikkaballapura district, on survey numbers 123 and 124/2. It was sanctioned by the DTCP in 2023 and the development is complete: 40 ft main road and 30 ft internal roads with kerbs, footpaths and covered drains, overhead tank, underground power and HT lines, a sewage treatment plant and rainwater harvesting.

Of the 440 sites, 298 are available for sale. Each has its own e-Swathu Form 9 Panchayat Khata and PID, so your sale deed registers at the Bagepalli sub-registrar in your name, with nothing pending.

Promoter: Honey Agro Farms. Marketed by AEVUM Ventures.

How a booking works
  1. Choose your site, get the cost sheet. Every line itemised: plot value, location charges, club and corpus, government charges. Usually within two hours.
  2. Book with Rs 1,00,000. Paid only to the AEVUM Ventures company account, by UPI, cheque or bank transfer, against a receipt. It holds the site for 48 hours.
  3. Agreement for sale, 10%. A registered agreement within the days agreed at booking; Club VIP membership, corpus and maintenance are paid at this stage.
  4. Second instalment, 40%. On the schedule in your agreement.
  5. Balance at registration. Sale deed at the Bagepalli sub-registrar, in your name, with the Khata transferred.

No cash, ever. Never pay any individual, including our staff.

Questions buyers ask

Before you visit.

Is the layout approved, and by whom?

Yes. The layout plan is sanctioned by the Directorate of Town and Country Planning, Chikkaballapura, under LAY(CBR)/03/2023, and the project is registered with Karnataka RERA under PRM/KA/RERA/1254/461/PR/270325/007630. The land is converted for residential use under ALN(SR)(CBR) 644/65 and 64/2012-13.

What is the Khata, and whose name is it in?

Every site has its own e-Swathu Form 9 Panchayat Khata with a PID, issued by the Paragodu Gram Panchayat. Today it stands in the promoter's name; on registration it is transferred to yours. You can check any PID on the e-Swathu portal before you book.

Can I get a bank loan?

Yes. The project is approved for loans by LIC Housing Finance, IDBI Bank, Axis Bank and ICICI Bank, so your sanction is faster and the title set is already with the bank. Site loans cover up to about 70 to 75% of the agreement value; the villa option can be financed as site plus construction. We hand one set of title documents to your bank's lawyer.

Is the development really finished?

Yes. Roads, drains, footpaths, overhead tank and water lines, underground power, STP and Club VIP are built and in use. The photographs on this page are of the site, not renders, except the two marked as renders in the gallery.

What does Rs 28.2 lakh include?

The plot value of a standard 30x40 site at the launch rate of Rs 2,349 per sq ft. Location charges (corner, east-facing, 12 m main road), Club VIP membership and society corpus, first-year maintenance, stamp duty and registration are extra and are shown line by line on the cost sheet.

When can I build?

As soon as the sale deed is registered. There is no waiting for infrastructure. Plan sanction for your house is through the Gram Panchayat.

How do site visits work?

Free, every day except Tuesday, with pick-up from Hebbal and Yelahanka on Saturdays and Sundays. The drive is about 1 hour 40 minutes from Hebbal up NH-44 with one toll at Devanahalli. Message us on WhatsApp for a slot.

Whom do I pay?

Only the AEVUM Ventures company account, by UPI, cheque or bank transfer, and every payment gets a receipt from AEVUM. Never pay any individual, including our staff, and never in cash.
